Example 1.2.3.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{8} + \frac{5}{6} - \frac{2}{3}\)
🔗
We could use \(8 \cdot 6 \cdot 3 = 144\) as a common denominator, but this is larger than necessary. Since \(8=2\cdot 2\cdot 2\) and \(6=3\cdot 2\text{,}\) and \(3\) is just \(3\text{,}\) we only need to include three \(2\)’s (since \(8\) has three of them) and one \(3\) (since that one three will cover for both the \(6\) and the \(3\) denominators). So our least common denominator is \(2\cdot 2\cdot 2\cdot 3=24\text{.}\) Then:
\begin{align*} \frac{3}{8} + \frac{5}{6} - \frac{2}{3} \amp = \frac{3}{8} \left( \frac{3}{3} \right) + \frac{5}{6} \left( \frac{4}{4} \right) - \frac{2}{3} \left( \frac{8}{8} \right)\\ \amp = \frac{9}{24} + \frac{20}{24} - \frac{16}{24}\\ \amp = \frac{13}{24} \end{align*}

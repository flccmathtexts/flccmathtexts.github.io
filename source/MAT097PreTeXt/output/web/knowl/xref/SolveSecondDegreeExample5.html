Example 3.2.7.

Factor \(x^2-5x+6\)
🔗
We need to consider all possible pairs of integers whose product is \(6\) and then look at their sum to see if it is possible to obtain a sum of the factors equal to \(-5\text{.}\)
🔗
We consider all possible pairs:
  • \(1\) and \(6\) (sum \(7\))
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(-1\) and \(-6\) (sum \(-7\))
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(2\) and \(3\) (sum \(5\))
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(-2\) and \(-3\) (sum \(-5\))
    🔗
    🔗
The last pair of factors are the factors that we want and \(x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)\text{.}\)

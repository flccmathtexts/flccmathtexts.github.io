Example 5.2.7.

The daily dose of insulin required by a diabetic is a linear function of the grams of carbohydrate consumed. For a particular patient, the daily dose (in units) as required for \(x\) grams of carbohydrate is given by the function \(f(x)=\frac{1}{6}x+18\text{.}\) What is the “starting amount” of insulin needed before even considering any carbohydrates consumed?
🔗
This “starting amount” is the vertical intercept. This would be the amount of insulin needed even if the patient consumed \(x=0\) grams. Substituting in we get:
\begin{equation*} f(0)=\frac{1}{6}(0)+18=18 \end{equation*}
🔗
So the vertical intercept is \(18\text{,}\) which we would interpret here as \(18\) units of insulin.
